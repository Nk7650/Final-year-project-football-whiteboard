    How to use Tactics Lab

    A quick tour of every feature. Press ? any time to reopen this.

    1. The basics

    • Demo match + plan loads a ready-made example so you can see everything working.
    • Move tool (V): drag a player or the ball to place them. New board clears the pitch; Reset all clears everything including names, profiles, cards, layers and settings.
    • Pick a formation for each team, and a team size from 3v3 to 11v11 (small sizes show a marked play area).

    2. Making a move (keyframes)

    • Set up the starting positions and press + Keyframe. Move players, press + Keyframe again. Repeat for each phase.
    • Click a Frame chip to jump back to a phase and edit it. Press Play to watch the players move between keyframes.
    • Use the speed menu (0.1x to 4x) or [ and ] to slow down or speed up.

    3. The ball

    • Select a keyframe that has another one after it, then use the Ball card: Flight (Ground, Lofted, Driven), Draw curve (or tool B, then click the pitch to bend the pass), Kick animation, and Ball carrier (the ball dribbles with one player).
    • These settings only work when the playhead is on a keyframe with a following keyframe.

    4. 3D view

    • Switch 3D / 2D at the top of the pitch. Choose a camera: Broadcast, Sideline, End, High, Tactical, Orbit, Follow or Drone (C cycles them).
    • Right-drag or Shift-drag to orbit, mouse wheel to zoom. The graphics menu lowers quality on slow computers.

    5. Drawing tools

    • R run arrow, P pass arrow, H shot line, Z zone, S spotlight a player, T text, E erase. Ctrl+Z undoes the last drawing; the bin button clears all drawings.

    6. Set pieces

    • In the Set pieces card choose Corner, Throw-in, Free kick, Goal kick or Penalty, the attacking team and the side, then Build. It creates phase keyframes you can play or edit.

    7. Player profiles, photos and cards

    • Click a player (or the i button in the lineup) to open their profile: name, position, age, height, foot, nationality, notes and a photo.
    • Match events: Yellow card, Red card (a second yellow becomes a red), goals, assists, subbed off and injured. They appear on the player's tag and in the Match events list. Red cards and subs remove the player from the pitch.

    8. Analysis

    • Heatmap: tick Show heatmap and choose whose movement to show. Analysis layers: team shape, defensive line, offside line, space control, trails, ball paths, shot lines. The Team shape table gives width, depth and back-line height in metres.

    9. Replay, plans and files

    • Load tracking / video replays real tracking data. Load plan overlay compares it with a planned shape and flags key moments. Export plan saves your keyframes as a plan.
    • Save board and Load board keep your work as a file. Snapshot PNG saves a picture. Present hides the editing panels for a clean view.

    10. Match clock, goals and the final whistle

    • The Match clock (Match tab) runs 0 to 90 minutes with optional added time after each half. It pauses at half time and full time. Drag the slider to jump to any minute.
    • When the ball crosses the goal line during a move, the board celebrates (GOAL banner, confetti, crowd roar) and updates the score. You can also press + Goal on a player's profile or the + Home / + Away goal buttons. The Sound button turns the crowd on or off.
    • In 3D, press Follow to choose the ball or any player for the camera to follow.
    • End match opens the full-time screen: score, goals, the best player based on the data, team stats, and a fans' vote you can run by tapping Vote on each player.
