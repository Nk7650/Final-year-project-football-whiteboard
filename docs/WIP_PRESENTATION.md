## Demo script (about 4 minutes)

Rehearse twice. Have the backup video ready.

1. Open `web/index.html`. Choose 4-3-3 vs 4-4-2. *"Width, depth and back line are computed live; 48, 53 and 25 metres for this shape."*
2. Add a keyframe, drag the back four up 15 m, add a second keyframe, press **Play**. *"This is the coach's plan: a stepped-up press."*
3. Click **Export plan as tracking** to save `plan-tracking.json`.
4. Click **Load tracking / video** and choose the real clip's `tracking.json`; load the MP4 beside it. *"This is the same pitch, now fed by tracked positions from video."*
5. Click **Load plan overlay**, choose the plan, and set **Plan starts at** to the right time. *"Dashed rings are the plan, solid players are what happened."*
6. Point at the deviation panel: *"The panel shows actual minus plan: the back line was X m higher than planned."*
7. In a terminal: `python -m tactics.compare plan-tracking.json tracking.json --offset <t> --threshold 5 --min-duration 2`. *"Every flag carries the numbers and the time, so a coach can check it against the video."*

**If something fails:** switch to the backup video, then to screenshots in `docs/img/`. Never debug live.

## Questions to expect, and where the answer is

| Question | Answer pointer |
|---|---|
| Who decides what a good formation is? | RESEARCH.md: the standard is the coach's plan, plus published metrics, reference data and expert validation |
| Why not detect players in the browser? | ARCHITECTURE.md, decision 1: accuracy and reproducibility |
| What is a homography and why four points? | A 3x3 matrix mapping image points to pitch points; it has 8 degrees of freedom and each point gives 2 equations, so four non-collinear points fix it exactly. See `tactics/geometry.py` |
| Why does calibration with four points tell you nothing about its own accuracy? | The fit is exact at those points; validate with held-out landmarks (EVALUATION.md section 2) |
| Why Hungarian matching in the evaluator? | One-to-one assignment minimising total distance avoids counting one detection for two players |
| What do precision and recall mean here? | Precision: share of detections matching an annotated player. Recall: share of annotated players that were detected, both within the gate distance |
| Why is the goalkeeper excluded from the metrics? | It would dominate depth and back-line height; the deepest player is used as the proxy (a stated limitation) |
| How do you know the board and the Python tools agree? | Both are tested against the same hand-computed numbers |
| What are the threats to validity? | RESEARCH.md: small samples, annotation bias, amateur vs professional benchmarks, one camera setup |
| What did you learn from the bugs? | PROJECT_LOG.md, problems and solutions |
| Where is the client? | (fill in once confirmed) |
| What does the AI do in your project? | AI_USE.md: the product uses pretrained vision models only; AI assisted development and is declared |
